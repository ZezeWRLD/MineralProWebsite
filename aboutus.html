Our Story

From Challenge to Solution

Mineral Pro Enterprise Pty Ltd is a South African & Global, 100% black female-owned entity specializing in comprehensive engineering material supply and plant support. Established in 2020 (Reg: 2020/772030/07), the company was founded to provide specialized, high-quality procurement and technical solutions adhering to stringent industry regulations and safety standards.

As a full B-BBEE contributor, Mineral Pro combines local industry expertise with a robust global reach. Equipped with international import and export licences and structured licensing support, we streamline the procurement of specialized equipment and materials from leading manufacturers worldwide.

2020 — Founded
